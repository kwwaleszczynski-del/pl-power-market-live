Method and data

Data

All series come from the public PSE reports API (api.raporty.pse.pl, no key required) in 15-minute resolution. Timestamps are converted to interval start and shown in Polish local time.

  • RCE (rce-pln): market price of electricity published by PSE for each quarter-hour of the next day. History panels and the battery model use $series.
  • SDAC (csdac-pln): price from the single day-ahead market coupling for the Polish zone.
  • Generation mix (his-wlk-cal): actual national demand, wind and PV output, other generation and cross-border exchange, updated during the day.
  • Forecast (pdgopkd): PSE day-ahead coordinated plan of demand, wind and PV.
  • Imbalance price CEN (crb-rozl): settlement price for imbalances, published the day after delivery.

Definitions

  • Daily spread: maximum minus minimum quarter-hour price of a delivery day.
  • Negative-price hours: number of quarter-hours with a price below zero, times 0.25 h.
  • Thermal & other: total generation (units in and outside the balancing market) minus wind and PV.
  • PV vs price: quarter-hours between 10:00 and 15:00 grouped in 1 GW bins of national PV output; median price with the interquartile range.

Battery model

$battery. Each delivery day is solved as a mixed-integer linear program (HiGHS) that chooses when to charge and discharge with perfect knowledge of that day's prices, using the optimiser of the companion project bess-arbitrage-pl. Day-ahead bids are submitted before the auction and the price is its result, so no operator knows these prices when bidding: this is the upper bound of pure day-ahead arbitrage, without bidding risk, balancing services, intraday trading or grid fees. With a forecast made before the gate a backtest earns about 83 % of it (pl-bess-paper-trading).

Refresh and resilience

A scheduled GitHub Actions workflow rebuilds the page every afternoon after PSE publishes next-day prices. Data are cached as monthly Parquet files; recent months are downloaded again on every run to pick up late publications and corrections. If the API does not answer, cached data are used and flagged below; if no price data are available at all, the previous version of the page stays online.
